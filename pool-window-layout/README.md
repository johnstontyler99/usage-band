# Pool alfresco gable – window layout concepts

Open `index.html` in a browser. It's an interactive, to-scale elevation for the glazed infill on the pool fence line
(11,844 wide, 3,110 plate, 9.7° raked ceiling), with three concepts:

- **Draft 2 – 4 windows + steel posts (current):** pivot doors in the centre, two windows each side with steel posts between them, at the door jambs and at each brick end; 1200 solid glass with screened louvres above; pool-side lobby with gate.
- **A – As sketched:** narrow/wide/narrow louvres either side of central double doors, with clad piers between.
- **B – Sketch rhythm, all glass:** the same rhythm with slim coupled frames and steel posts at the door jambs.
- **C – Fence-line grid (first draft):** 12 × 987 modules, a fixed glass base to the 1200 fence line, louvres above, double pivot doors opening into a glass-fenced pool-side lobby with a self-closing gate, and gable posts on the grid.

Sliders update the elevation, the photo overlay and the opening schedule. Static snapshots: `elev_A.png`, `elev_B.png`, `elev_draft2.png`, `plan_draft2.png`, `mont_draft2.png` (Draft 2); `elev_A.png`, `elev_B.png` (earlier options).

Drawing pack: `Pool-gable-infill_Drawing-pack_Rev-B.pdf` (A3, 6 sheets: DA-00 cover, DA-01 elevation 1:50, DA-02 plan 1:50, DA-03 lobby detail 1:20, DA-04 opening schedule, steel setout and notes, DA-05 window & door schedule).
