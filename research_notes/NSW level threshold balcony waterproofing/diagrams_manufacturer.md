# Manufacturer and supplier details for level-threshold tiled balconies (Australia, NSW focus), with collected diagrams

Scope: manufacturer and supplier technical details only (authority and standards diagrams are covered by another researcher). Research date: 25 November 2026. All diagram files are in `diagrams/manufacturer/`, next to this file. Manufacturer statements about compliance are recorded as **claims**, not verified facts. "Level threshold" here means the finished external surface is flush, or nearly flush, with the internal floor, so there is no visible step-down.

Overall picture: no Australian membrane manufacturer I checked publishes a detail called "level threshold" or "flush threshold" for a tiled balcony. The public details that show the finished surfaces level come from drainage suppliers (Stormtech, Aquabocci) and door suppliers (AWS, Alspec). Ardex Australia's large public CAD library shows door terminations with a step-down only. The European "barrier-free" details (Schlüter) and the pedestal details (Viking Buzon, NZ) get a flush finish by keeping the **membrane** low and lifting the **finish**.

## 1. Membrane manufacturers' published level-threshold, flush-threshold or "no set-down" door details

### Takeaway
Ardex Australia publishes the most complete free set of door-opening details: CAD drawings D46–D48, D68–D70 and D210–D211, as PDF and DWG. Every one of them shows the external finished floor **below** the internal floor, with the step height referred to AS 4654.2-2012 Appendix A Table A1. None is a level threshold. The only Ardex details usable for a flush finish are a generic drainage-channel detail (D55) and pedestal details (D66/D67), and neither shows the door. I found no public level-threshold balcony details from Mapei AU, Davco/Sika AU, Tremco AU, Wet Seal, Gripset, Bostik, Soprema AU, Duraproof or Kemper. Schlüter's (UK/EU, DIN-based) balcony manual is the only membrane-system document found with an explicit barrier-free (<2 cm) door detail.

### Inferences
- In Australia, Ardex's public details implicitly treat the step-down/waterstop arrangement (height per AS 4654.2 Table A1) as the default. A designer wanting a level threshold with Ardex products would have to combine D55 (channel drain) and/or D66 (pedestal) with a threshold-drain or door-supplier detail, and should get Ardex technical services to sign off the combination.
- Schlüter's EU documents show the logic used for level thresholds: the membrane stays low, the finish is raised on drainage layers, and a channel at the door replaces the upstand height. They label it a "special construction" needing owner approval. They are DIN-based and are **not** evidence of AS 4654.2 compliance.

### Gaps
- No public level-threshold balcony detail was found for Mapei AU, Davco/Sika AU, Tremco AU, Gripset, Bostik, Soprema AU, Duraproof, Kemper or Wet Seal. Mapei's site blocked automated access (403). Kemper and Duraproof were not reached.
- I could not confirm whether Schlüter TROBA-LINE, DITRA-DRAIN or KERDI-LINE are distributed in Australia, or whether any Schlüter document addresses AS 4654.2.
- Ardex drawings cite AS 4654.2-**2012**. I did not check whether a newer edition has replaced it (outside my scope; for the standards researcher).

## 2. Threshold and channel drain suppliers' balcony door threshold details

### Takeaway
Stormtech (South Nowra, NSW) has the most complete free Australian level-threshold drain documentation. There are two families: **TDi** (a drain with a separate door-track sub-sill; the membrane is completed first and bonded to the drain flange) and **TDiS** (the drain tray *is* the sub-sill for "sill-only" doors). Published dimensions include a tray depth of 23–53 mm, overall depth up to 120 mm, minimum channel length equal to the door track length + 6 mm, and a claimed "minimum 50 mm stepdown within the channel". AWS FlowTHRU/FlowTHRU Pro (a door-integrated trough) and Aquabocci R-47 Low Profile (with an EPDM sleeve under the door track) are alternatives. Their public material has far fewer dimensions.

### Inferences
- The Stormtech details show two different waterproofing approaches. TDi uses a continuous membrane under everything, bonded to the drain flange, with the door track and drain sitting "above and separate to" it. TDiS treats the drain tray and integrated sub-sill as the water-collecting element, with the membrane under and around it. Either way, the structural slab needs a **local rebate**: roughly the door track or sill depth (≥30–35 mm) plus tray depth (23–53 mm), and up to 120 mm overall at the drain. The rest of the balcony slab only needs set-down for membrane, screed/bed and tile.
- Stormtech's "minimum 50 mm stepdown within the channel" claim presents the drain itself as providing the step that would otherwise be a structural step-down. Whether a certifier accepts that is a compliance question for the standards researcher, not a fact established here.
- Common requirements across drain suppliers: drain length ≥ door track length (Stormtech: +6 mm); an overflow outlet (Stormtech); a sealed or EPDM interface between the door track and the drain (Aquabocci); dissimilar-metal separation (Stormtech); and grate slots ≤6 mm (AWS) or ≤13 mm (AS 1428.1 per Alspec, §3).

### Gaps
- None of the drain documents found states how far the channel must extend beyond the door jambs. Stormtech gives only "door track length + 6 mm".
- No AWS FlowTHRU trough depth or set-down figure is publicly accessible (behind login, or the old PDFs return 404).
- I found no public detail or dimensions for Allgrate, Lauxes, ACO AU, Hydro Channel, Schlüter KERDI-LINE (AU) or Tilera threshold drains.
- Stormtech's warranty document ([Care + Warranty](https://stormtech.com.au/documents/Stormtech-Care-and-Warranty.pdf)) covers finish care and handling. I found no threshold- or waterproofing-specific warranty conditions in it.

## 3. Door system suppliers' level/flush sill details, sill trays and AS 2047 ratings

### Takeaway
AWS (Elevate/Vantage) and Alspec publish flush or recessed-sill sliding doors with stated AS 2047 water ratings. AWS SlideMASTER Recessed Sill (Series 703): 600 Pa in the table, "600–1200 Pa" in the text. Alspec ProGlide UltraFlat: 630 Pa. ## 4. Pedestal and raised-floor systems and drainage mats (flush finish, low membrane)

### Takeaway
Pedestal details all keep the membrane on the structural slab and raise the paver on adjustable pedestals, so the paver top can match the internal FFL. The best door-threshold pedestal detail found is NZ's Viking Buzon BZ03: membrane turned up 100 mm minimum at the door, 12 mm minimum gap between paver and joinery, structural step ≥100 mm under NZ E2/AS1. Ardex Australia's D66/D67 show pedestals over Ardex membranes but do not show the door. Buzon's lowest pedestal adjusts from 12 to 18 mm. I found no Eterno or Wallbarn Australian door details.

### Cited Findings
- **Ardex D66 "Pedestal system with Ardex liquid membrane"** (17/06/2026). Layers: substrate, then Ardex primer, then Ardex liquid membrane (WPM 909, WPM 157 or WPM 002), then DTA BOSS pedestal pad, then DTA BOSS pedestal, then paver, with silicone at the edge. The membrane is continuous and unbroken under the pedestals. The door interface is not shown. — [Ardex D66 PDF](https://www.ardexaustralia.com/pdf/cad/D66_PEDESTAL%20SYSTEM%20WITH%20ARDEX%20LIQUID%20MEMBRANE.pdf); [D66 page](https://ardexaustralia.com/cad-library/d66-pedestal-system-with-ardex-liquid-membrane/)
- **Ardex D67 "Pedestal system with Ardex WELDTEC membrane"**: the same build-up with WPM 715/1000/1500/Root Repell sheet membranes and Ardex WA 98 adhesive. — [Ardex D67 PDF](https://www.ardexaustralia.com/pdf/cad/D67_PEDESTAL%20SYSTEM%20WITH%20ARDEX%20WELDTEC%20MEMBRANE.pdf)
- **Viking Buzon (NZ) "Typical detail for Door Threshold BZ03"** (13/06/2016, "information only", "substrate indicative only"):
  - Section: the interior floor level is at the same height as the top of the pavers.

  - Membrane: "Turn-up Membrane 100mm minimum at Trimmer Studs", running up behind the aluminium joinery with an air seal.
  - "12mm minimum gap required" between the paver edge and the aluminium joinery.
  - Note: "Substrate to comply with current Masterspec Specifications…".
  - This is a **New Zealand** document (E2/AS1, Masterspec) and is included only to illustrate the method.
  - [Viking Buzon details set, p.3](https://www.vikingroofspec.co.nz/media/j43n2enf/viking-buzon-details-full-set.pdf)
- **Buzon PB-0-S12** technical data sheet: "Adjustable from 12 to 18 mm". "With addition of U-PH5 slope corrector, the PB-S is adjustable in height from 26 to 32 mm and slope from 0 to 5%." Head Ø170 mm, base Ø197 mm. — [Buzon PB-0-S12 TDS](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0580/8495/0152/files/Technical_Data_Sheet_-_BUZON_-_PB-0-S12.pdf)

Pasco Construction Solutions is reported as the exclusive Australian agent. — [Archify – Buzon AU](https://www.archify.com/au/product/buzon) (search-result summary); [Viking Buzon PDS](https://www.vikingroofspec.co.nz/media/2sxnxaxs/viking-buzon-pedestal-product-data-sheet-v1-1-2026.pdf) (search-result summary: heights to 1 m with couplers, 1000 kg capacity)
- Elmich pedestals are marketed as "adjustable from 12 mm" and allowing access back to the membrane. — [Outdoor Design – raising the game](https://outdoordesign.com.au/landscape-structures/decking/raising-the-game-in-paving-and-timber-decks/676) (search-result summary)
- **Drainage mats / bonded drainage (Schlüter, EU)**: DITRA-DRAIN 4/8, TROBA-PLUS and BEKOTEC-DRAIN layers over the waterproofing, with a TROBA-LINE channel at the door (see §1, p.12 and p.30). DITRA-DRAIN is recommended for coverings 30 × 30 cm or larger. — [Schlüter Heft A](https://eu.schluter.com/statics/www_schlueter_gb_2x/downloads/cumulus/551852_Heft_A_Balkone_GB.pdf)

### Inferences
- With pedestals, the level threshold is achieved because the finish floats above a membrane that stays low and fully turned up at the door. Water then reaches the membrane at the door line, so the upturn behind or under the door frame and the sub-sill seal carry the waterproofing. That is why NZ BZ03 keeps a 100 mm structural step and 100 mm upturn even with a flush finish.
- Buzon's 12–18 mm minimum pedestal plus a typical 20 mm porcelain paver gives about 32–40 mm minimum from membrane to finished level. Adding the 0–5 % slope corrector gives about 46–52 mm, before any fall in the substrate. This is my arithmetic from the TDS, not a manufacturer statement.

### Gaps
- No Australian (AS 4654.2-referenced) pedestal-at-door detail was found from Buzon/Pasco, DTA, Eterno, Wallbarn or Elmich. Eterno and Wallbarn Australian material was not located.
- No Australian distributor information was found for Schlüter drainage mats.

## 5. Typical required build-up depth (structural set-down) per approach

### Takeaway
Published manufacturer dimensions show the local depth needed at the door line for the drain-based approaches. For pedestals, they show the depth needed from the membrane to finished level. Total slab set-down also depends on the falls over the balcony length, which none of these documents states.

### Cited Findings
- **Stormtech TDiS** (sill-only): overall drain depth = door track depth + 6.2 mm + tray depth (23–53 mm), up to 120 mm. Minimum door track depth is 30 mm (100 grate) or 35 mm (65 grate). Claimed "min 50mm stepdown in channel" from internal FFL. — [Stormtech sill-only order form](https://stormtech.com.au/documents/Premium-Threshold-Sill-Only.pdf); [100TDiS sheet](https://stormtech.com.au/documents/100TDiS-threshold.pdf)
- **Stormtech TDi** (separate sub-sill): sill depth min 30 mm (100 grate) or 35 mm (65 grate), plus tray depth 23–53 mm. The tray must be "slightly deeper than the lowest part of the sub sill". — [Stormtech TDi order form](https://stormtech.com.au/documents/Stormtech_Threshold_Order_Form_Updated_Diagram.pdf); [TDi sheet](https://stormtech.com.au/documents/TDi-threshold-waterproofing_3.pdf)
- **AWS FlowTHRU Pro**: "single level set down", suited to slabs "where slab thickness is limited". No depth is published openly. — [AWS FlowTHRU Pro](https://awsaustralia.com.au/complementary/flowthru-pro-drain/)
- **AWS SlideMASTER recessed sill**: the door track is recessed into the slab, with removable floating tiles over it. Depth not published on the sheet. — [AWS 703 sheet](https://www.specifyaws.com.au/cdn/lhjUiWS3K4Cy/703-productsheet.pdf)
- **Pedestal** (Buzon): pedestal 12–18 mm (26–32 mm with slope corrector) plus paver. NZ BZ03 still uses a ≥100 mm structural step at the door. — [Buzon PB-0-S12](https://cdn.shopify.com/s/files/1/0580/8495/0152/files/Technical_Data_Sheet_-_BUZON_-_PB-0-S12.pdf); [Viking BZ03](https://www.vikingroofspec.co.nz/media/j43n2enf/viking-buzon-details-full-set.pdf)
- **Conventional step-down (Ardex D69/D70)**: the internal-to-external FFL step is set "per AS 4654.2-2012 Appendix A Table A1"; no figure is printed on the drawing. — [Ardex D70](https://www.ardexaustralia.com/pdf/cad/D70_TERMINATION%20AT%20EXTERNAL%20DOOR%20OPENINGS%20NO%20SUB%20SILL%20-%20ARDEX%20LIQUID%20MEMBRANE.pdf)
- **Schlüter (DIN)**: a channel at the door reduces the threshold from 15 cm to 5 cm. A barrier-free threshold under 2 cm uses two channels and a slope under 6 % near the door. — [Schlüter p.29–30](https://eu.schluter.com/statics/www_schlueter_gb_2x/downloads/cumulus/551852_Heft_A_Balkone_GB.pdf)

### Inferences
- **Membrane + bed + tile with a threshold drain (Stormtech-type)**:
  - Local rebate at the door of roughly 55–120 mm below internal FFL to fit the drain (e.g. 30–35 mm sill depth + 23–53 mm tray, more with the PVC packer and outlet fittings).
  - Away from the door, the slab set-down must cover membrane + bed/screed + tile + falls.
  - Typical bed + tile is about 20–50 mm. Falls are 1:100 per the vendor blog above. Both figures are my inference, not from these manufacturers.
- **Membrane + pedestal + paver**: roughly 32–52 mm minimum from membrane to finished level at the shallowest pedestal, increasing with substrate falls. The door upturn still needs depth, as BZ03 shows.

### Gaps
- No Australian manufacturer publishes a total "structural set-down" figure for a level-threshold tiled balcony. All figures above are component depths.

## 6. Manufacturer warranties and installation conditions specific to level thresholds

### Takeaway
No manufacturer document found offers a warranty specific to level thresholds. The documents do state installation conditions: install the sub-sill before the door, seal before drilling fixings (Ardex); complete waterproofing before fitting the drain and sub-sill, separate dissimilar metals, provide an overflow (Stormtech); obtain owner approval for barrier-free constructions (Schlüter EU). Product warranties are general and "on request" (Ardex).

### Cited Findings
- Ardex: "1. Sub-sill is installed before door. 2. Seal between sill, packer and membrane before drilling fixing holes." - Schlüter (EU): barrier-free door assemblies are "special constructions" that "require the prior approval of the owner." — [Schlüter p.30](https://eu.schluter.com/statics/www_schlueter_gb_2x/downloads/cumulus/551852_Heft_A_Balkone_GB.pdf)
- AWS: product sheets are "general in nature and… not sufficient for the specification or manufacture of AWS products. Refer to your AWS Fabricator." — [AWS 703 sheet](https://www.specifyaws.com.au/cdn/lhjUiWS3K4Cy/703-productsheet.pdf)
- Alspec: confirm the door "has been independently tested to meet the requirements for operational force as well as air infiltration and water penetration." — [Alspec whitepaper](https://alspec.com.au/wp-content/uploads/2024/01/2020-alspec-over-the-threshold-whitepaper-3.pdf)

### Inferences
- In practice, the warranty position for a level threshold depends on using a tested door and drain combination (AWS FlowTHRU + Vantage/Elevate) or a membrane-to-drain interface the membrane manufacturer accepts (e.g. Ardex D55-type tape-to-flange). Project-specific sign-off from the membrane and drain manufacturers is the prudent route.

### Gaps
- I found no published level-threshold-specific warranty terms from any manufacturer. Stormtech's Care + Warranty document covers finish care only.
